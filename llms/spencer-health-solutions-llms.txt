## Products

- spencer® — countertop in-home smart medication dispenser. Dispenses pharmacy-prepared,
  pre-loaded multi-dose refills on schedule with audio and visual alerts. Reported 97% adherence.
- spencerAssist® — mobile app for patients and caregivers: medication schedules, health readings,
  real-time dispense alerts.
- spencerCare™ — web portal for healthcare professionals: real-time alerts, telehealth visits,
  survey and biometric data collection.
- spencer SmartHub™ — the clinical-trial configuration, used as an in-home trial partner for
  decentralized trials.
- Real-world data collection — recurring in-device surveys plus paired Bluetooth biometric devices.
